Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Job Application Assistant is built and run by Jonathan Mimnaugh. It compares your resume to a job posting and writes a tailored resume and cover letter. This policy covers the website and the Chrome extension.

In short: your resume and the job posting are sent to our server and to an AI provider to produce your results, and then they're discarded. We don't keep them, sell them, or use them for anything else.

What you send us

When you click Analyze, the app sends our server:

  • Your resume, as pasted text or an uploaded PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.
  • The job posting, as pasted text, an uploaded file, or a link.
  • The cover letter tone you picked.

When you download a resume or cover letter, the app sends the text you're downloading so the server can turn it into a PDF or DOCX file.

What happens to it

  • Our server reads the text out of your files and sends your resume and the job posting to Groq, the AI provider that scores the match and writes your tailored resume and cover letter.
  • If you give a link, our server downloads that page to find the job description.
  • The results are sent back to your browser. Our server doesn't save your resume, the job posting, or your results. It has no database, and nothing you send is written to disk.
  • Like most web servers, ours records basic technical details of each request, such as your IP address, the time, and which page was requested. Our hosting provider keeps these logs for a limited time. We also hold your IP address briefly in memory to limit how many requests one person can make per minute.

The Chrome extension

  • Saved resume. After your first successful analysis, the extension saves your resume in your browser, on your computer only. It's never stored on our server. Click Remove saved resume to delete it, or uninstall the extension, which deletes it too.
  • Reading job pages. When you click Use this job page, the extension reads the job description from the tab you're on and puts it in the job posting box. It reads the page only when you click that button. It doesn't track your browsing or run in the background on the sites you visit.
  • Permissions. The extension can read LinkedIn and Indeed job pages, and any other page only after you click its toolbar icon on that page. It uses these permissions only to read job descriptions when you ask.

Error reports

When something breaks, the app sends a report to Sentry so we can fix it. Reports include technical details such as the error message, the browser and operating system, and which part of the app failed. They're set up to leave out your resume, the job posting, your results, and your IP address.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone beyond the providers named here.
  • We don't use your information for advertising.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking on the website or in the extension.
  • We don't use your resume or job postings to train AI models.
  • We don't ask you to create an account.

Who else handles your information

  • Groq processes your resume and the job posting to generate results.
  • Render hosts our server.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Sentry receives error reports.

Each handles information under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
